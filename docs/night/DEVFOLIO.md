# Pay-on-Proof — Devfolio submission draft

**Outcome-based payments on Cardano, released against signed API3 data.** Pay-on-Proof is a testnet prototype for paying a creator or an agent when an independently reported outcome meets a rule agreed in advance. **Receipt:** [project overview](../../README.md), [policy engine](../../aiken/airnode_spike/lib/engine.ak), [spend validator](../../aiken/airnode_spike/validators/pay_on_proof.ak).

**Evidence boundary:** This draft describes source and historical receipts at commit `82d88dc63341e8498b4728e3bd2f3996aa560a5e`. “Recorded” means the repository reports a result; it does not mean job K reran it. One recorded payout was independently inspected in Chrome; the verification record below states the exact coverage. No new transaction, signature, token expenditure or funded API call was made.

## The problem

A brand wants to pay a creator for a measurable result, while the creator wants to know the payment is already committed. The same problem appears when a buyer hires a software agent for a task whose outcome can be measured outside the agent. Our proposed answer is a funded escrow with a public settlement rule, rather than either party deciding afterwards whether to pay. These are target use cases, not validated customer demand. **Receipt:** [trust model, sections 1 and 3](../../TRUST.md), [customer hypotheses and absence of interviews](../../ITERATIONS.md#what-we-do-not-know).

An unsigned count supplied by the person collecting the money would let that person invent the outcome. Pay-on-Proof makes the contract authenticate the response itself before applying the payment rule. It still trusts the selected data source: a signature authenticates a reported fact, not its truth or the quality of an agent's work. **Receipt:** [attestation verification](../../aiken/airnode_spike/lib/airnode.ak), [release checks](../../aiken/airnode_spike/lib/engine.ak), [trust boundaries](../../TRUST.md#1-what-the-contract-proves).

## What we built

- **Conditional escrow on Cardano preprod.** An Aiken/Plutus V3 validator checks signed data, pays the named beneficiary on a valid release, and permits a refund authorized by the named refund party from the deadline. - **An x402 route that pays into the escrow.** The buyer client recomputes the offered policy before paying; the payment output carries the inline datum at the validator. **Receipt:** [escrow offers](../../cardano/src/escrow.ts), [buyer checks](../../cardano/src/escrow-client.ts); recorded current-version payment `5d3488232e4944a7600c69c07558f448e9750fd5468126d80c44ff077b970923` and keeper release `aba3ded929b15142c0804a09c449c8027d11a43bf522c33a80d7531e6868bd94`, also in [the keeper log](../../cardano/out/autorelease-log-v2-test.jsonl).

Deletion was simulated by removing a like, not by deleting the post. **Receipt:** [intake](../../cardano/src/creator-intake.ts), [campaign planner](../../cardano/src/creator-campaign.ts), [tranche log](../../cardano/out/autorelease-log-tranches.jsonl); payout `3f22b3451cdccfcbea302b61221f06c25e9160f930d436edf421cc2bdf3426a3`, refunds `201484c1032ef4cbba34e25f609da1dec0a5198388497bee3aa636fe2da502d9` and `c5f8d5ca488b0bc4754b4ac4f14c55a2d0a88cb6073db1ce1618e872b0f4703d` in [the recorded scenario](../../PROOFS.md#tranches-on-chain-a-campaign-payout-to-a-named-payee-and-a-retention-part-that-did-not-pay-30-oct-2026-1900-to-1940-utc).
- **A keeper with repeated cycles, a data budget, scheduling and backoff.** It discovers eligible escrows, chooses when to check data, and attempts release when the policy holds. An MCP server exposes contract tools; a Masumi MIP-003 adapter exposes the keeper as a service, but real Masumi settlement is not verified. **Receipt:** [judge instructions](../../cardano/JUDGES.md), [address-only host](../../cardano/src/judge-site.ts), [MCP implementation](../../cardano/src/mcp-server.ts), [Masumi implementation](../../cardano/src/masumi-agent.ts), [stand-in-only test disclosure](../../PROOFS.md#the-keeper-as-a-masumi-agent-stand-in-tested-30-oct-2026-about-2000-to-2110-utc).

## How it works

1. **Agree and fund.** The inline policy pins the Airnode public key, request hash, signed-text structure, numeric rule, time limits, payout, beneficiary and refund party. The buyer client checks the offer against its own reconstruction. **Receipt:** [Policy type and encoding](../../cardano/src/engine.ts), [offer verification](../../cardano/src/escrow-client.ts).
2. **Bring the proof.** A keeper obtains the signed API response and supplies its timestamp, numeric slots and signature. The Cardano contract does not call the API. Data acquisition for paid sources is separate from Cardano settlement and historically used aUSD on Base Sepolia. **Receipt:** [proof helpers](../../cardano/src/proof.ts), [Airnode helper](../../lib/airnode-v2.ts), [recorded data payments](../../PROOFS.md#base-sepolia-airnodehub-calls-paid-in-ausd-x402).
3. **Verify, then apply the rule.** The engine reconstructs the signed text; the attestation library checks secp256k1 against the pinned key over an EIP-191 hash of the request hash, timestamp and data. The engine checks signature age, measurement window, deadline and a numeric sum against a threshold. Observation freshness is optional and only usable for feeds with a signed observation time. **Receipt:** [message_hash and verify_attestation](../../aiken/airnode_spike/lib/airnode.ak), [release_ok and observation_ok](../../aiken/airnode_spike/lib/engine.ak).
4. **Settle or refund.** A release may spend exactly one input of this validator and must pay the beneficiary at least the locked lovelace and stated payout. A refund requires the refund party in the required-signers list and the deadline to have arrived; the ledger enforces actual transaction signatures. The spent UTxO cannot pay twice, although a response can serve other matching policies. **Receipt:** [validator](../../aiken/airnode_spike/validators/pay_on_proof.ak), [replay and signature caveats](../../TRUST.md#4-known-gaps).

## Users and value

Our first customer hypothesis is brands and creator agencies: agree the post, the measurement time and the threshold before funding, then release against the signed measurement. Creators can inspect that funds and a payee are committed. This is a proposed benefit, not evidence of willingness to pay. **Receipt:** [Creator Pay design](../../cardano/CREATOR_PAY.md), [unvalidated customer hypotheses](../../ITERATIONS.md#what-we-do-not-know).

A second hypothesis is buyers and sellers of outcome-based agent jobs, with Masumi as a potential distribution channel. The prototype separates the keeper's service payment from the creator's conditional payout. The service model is to charge for a successful keeper job and eventually campaign tooling; commercial viability is not verified. **Receipt:** [Masumi integration boundary](../../cardano/MASUMI.md), [service model and costs](../../SERVICE.md).

## The three API3 questions

The following headings paraphrase the three explanation topics in the official [API3 challenge requirements](https://api3dao.github.io/airnodehub-hackathon/docs/#requirements), observed in Chrome during job K. The linked files and hashes are the project receipts for the answers.

### 1. Who depends on the data?

The buyer depends on the reported outcome to avoid paying against a fabricated result; the creator or agent depends on it to unlock the committed payment. The keeper transports the proof, while the validator authenticates it and applies the rule. Both parties still rely on the pinned Airnode key and upstream source. **Receipt:** [trust model](../../TRUST.md#1-what-the-contract-proves), [policy checks](../../aiken/airnode_spike/lib/engine.ak), recorded named-payee payout `3f22b3451cdccfcbea302b61221f06c25e9160f930d436edf421cc2bdf3426a3` in [the tranche log](../../cardano/out/autorelease-log-tranches.jsonl).

### 2. What fails if someone forges the data?

If an untrusted messenger could change a count, substitute another request or signer, or replay an overly old response, the buyer could lose the escrow without the agreed evidence. The implementation pins those checks on chain. The repository records a bench with accepted controls around four rejected tamper cases; these are provider script evaluations before submission, not four failed transactions. Job K did not rerun that bench. **Receipt:** [attestation tamper tests](../../aiken/airnode_spike/lib/airnode.ak), [time checks](../../aiken/airnode_spike/lib/engine.ak), [tamper fixture files](../../cardano/vectors/tamper), [judge procedure](../../cardano/JUDGES.md), [recorded current bench results](../../PROOFS.md#more-templates-through-the-paid-escrow-route-the-consoles-live-runs-and-a-mistake-30-oct-2026-about-1535-to-1610-utc).

Authentically signed misinformation, bought likes or a compromised trusted signer remain outside this protection. We do not claim a signature proves genuine engagement. **Receipt:** [remaining trust and oracle risks](../../TRUST.md#4-known-gaps).

### 3. Which desired data could we not obtain?

We wanted fresh counters for arbitrary creator posts across platforms. The recorded LunarCrush exploration obtained a signed YouTube snapshot but found unchanged counts across two observations and coverage restricted to indexed posts; the Instagram listing returned aggregate interactions rather than the separate view/like counters wanted for that workflow. Consequently, we do not claim arbitrary live YouTube/Instagram campaigns work. This is a report of those experiments, not a claim that the entire AirnodeHub catalogue lacks such data today. **Receipt:** [recorded LunarCrush exploration and limitations](../../PROOFS.md#lunarcrush-through-airnodehub-a-youtube-view-count-against-the-contract-30-oct-2026-1728-to-1731-utc), [saved projected vector](../../cardano/vectors/lunar_youtube.json), historical acquisition transaction `0xa1aed4509c7484985266e922d1da54e26d2312b7666a8ee8d4427444e7756637`.
