## Commercial and product validation still open

The [economics morning handoff](https://github.com/luk-huebner/tum-hack-2026/blob/night/mentor-economics/docs/night/mentor-economics/MORNING_HANDOFF.md) and [PR12](https://github.com/luk-huebner/tum-hack-2026/pull/12) are completed research, not completed company validation.

- **Customer demand and buyer work saved:** agency/creator interviews and a time diary are still needed. Public competitor prices are not proof of our willingness to pay or avoidable labor savings.
- **Commercial signed-data price/terms:** test aUSD/tADA receipts and direct upstream API cash prices do not establish the cost of a production authenticated response. Obtain supported pricing, quotas, freshness and signer provenance/rotation terms.
- **Billing policy:** agree what monitoring was delivered, unused reserve refunds, our-service failure credits and exact success-fee triggers, especially partial tranches. Creator principal is not our revenue. These terms and billing are proposals, not implemented product features.
- **Capacity and profitability:** measure repeat looks, sequential release capacity, deadline cohorts, support time and actual payment/platform fees. Synthetic scenarios and a single demo payout are not throughput benchmarks, a service-level promise or positive unit economics.
- **Operating a real-money service:** independent security review, controlled key operations, durable recovery, access/tenant isolation, support/dispute arrangements and relevant professional legal/accounting review remain prerequisites. No production-safety, compliance, incorporation or commercial-eligibility conclusion is supplied here.

## Review and closure rules

1. Inspect the actual PR branch and tested commit, not just main or the PR title. Draft and merge status can change; this index is a dated snapshot.
2. A team owner should record the closing commit, command/version/result, receipt or explicit business decision for each item. Proposed fixes, documentation and mock results are not equivalent closure evidence.
3. Distinguish **already corrected**, **confirmed at old base / current main needs recheck**, **partial**, **blocked**, **not verified** and **future product hypothesis**. Avoid presenting every open question as a security flaw.
4. Merge/release decisions stay with the team. This checklist changes no running code and authorizes no purchases, signatures, transactions, credential handling or external outreach.
